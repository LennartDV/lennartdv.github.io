GAME RULES

SPECIAL CARDS

  • 2: Attack (+2 Cards). Can be countered by 2, Joker, or matching Ace.
  • Joker: Wild Attack (+5 Cards). Play on anything. Can counter 2s.
  • 7: Play Again. stick to your turn.
  • 8: Skip Next Player. Counter with 8 to pass it on.
  • Ace: Reverse Direction. Reflects attacks if used to counter.

BASICS

  • Match SUIT or RANK.
  • Joker is the exception: Play on anything (except attacking Ace).
  • Ace Defense: Can only counter a 2 if Suit matches.
  • Ace Attack: If attacked by Ace, you MUST play another Ace or draw. Joker cannot defend Ace.
