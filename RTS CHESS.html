CONTROLS
L-CLICK : Select / Place Unit / Auto-Attack Enemy
R-CLICK : Move To / Attack Target / Delete (Setup)

0-9 : Save Squad (if selected) / Load Squad (if empty)
Q / E : Rotate Camera (Battle Phase)
UNITS
ARCHER: Ranged, Low HP. Good support.
SWORD: Melee, Medium HP. Frontline.
KNIGHT: Fast Melee, High HP. Flanker.
GAMEPLAY
1. SETUP: Spend 40 points to build your army. You can only build on the first 3 rows.
2. BATTLE: Units fight automatically if idle. You can override them with R-Click commands.
3. STRATEGY: R-Click selected units to open the Strategy Menu (Spike, Split, Surround).

GOAL: Eliminate all enemy units.
