Surgeon General Declares "Holiday Rom-Coms" a Class 1 Neuro-Hazard

"The brain cannot survive that much plaid," claims shocking new report from the Global Institute of Neural Preservation.

By Dr. A. I. Turing | Dateline: Switzerland | 3 Min Read

In a stunning press conference that has left the world reeling (and frantically canceling Disney+ subscriptions), the Global Institute of Neural Preservation (GINP) has released a study confirming that watching Christmas movies causes rapid, reversible brain shrinkage.

Conversely, the study suggests that bing-watching Science Fiction is the only known cure for what doctors are calling "Festive Atrophy Syndrome."

The "Gingerbread Effect"

According to lead researcher Dr. Cornelius Cortex, the human brain enters a "hibernation state" the moment it detects a plot involving a high-powered business woman returning to her hometown to save a family bakery.

"We observed the subjects watching 'The Knight Before Christmas'. Within 15 minutes, their critical thinking synapses coated themselves in a sticky, metaphorical mental nog. This causes the frontal lobe to turn into jelly. We call this the Gingerbread Effect."
— Dr. Cornelius Cortex, GINP Lead Researcher

The report outlines the specific dangers of common holiday tropes:

  • The "Magic Santa" Trope: Lowers math skills by 40%.
  • The "Grumpy Prince" Trope: Causes temporary loss of object permanence.
  • Excessive Tinsel Exposure: Over-stimulates the visual cortex, leading to an inability to perceive the color beige.

The Equation of Doom

The researchers presented a terrifying equation regarding the rate of decline:

$$ IQ_{loss} = \frac{\text{CandyCanes} \times \sqrt{\text{Jolliness}}}{\text{PlotComplexity}} $$

As PlotComplexity approaches zero (as seen in most cable holiday movies), IQ loss approaches infinity.

The Cure: Lasers and Paradoxes

However, there is hope. The same study found that Science Fiction acts as "cross-fit for the neurons." While a holiday movie comforts the brain into a coma, Sci-Fi forces the brain to perform mental gymnastics.

Metric Christmas Movie Sci-Fi Movie
Brain Activity Flatline (Comatose Bliss) Hyper-Active (Dread)
Logic Centers Disabled by "Magic" Overclocked by AI Ethics
Result You forget your PIN code You understand Quantum Mechanics

"If you find yourself wondering if the small-town innkeeper is actually Santa Claus," Dr. Cortex warned, "Turn off the TV immediately and watch a cyborg question the nature of its own soul. It’s the only way to save your mind."
